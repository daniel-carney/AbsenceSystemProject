Absence Notification System

Effective communication, WITHOUT THE 'FLUFF' !

A modern, easy way to notify your professors of an absence.

1

Install Extension

Add the tool to your browser once. It stays pinned to your toolbar for all current and future courses.

2

Enter Course Key

Type in the unique key found in your course syllabus. The system remembers this for you.

3

Submit Notice

Complete all required fields in the popup form and click submit to record your absence.

4

Confirm Receipt

Check your university email immediately for an automated receipt confirming your submission.

Streamlined for Students

Stop Writing Emails

No more stressing over how to phrase a formal email. Just fill out the form and you're done in seconds.

Instant Peace of Mind

The automated receipt serves as your digital "paper trail," proving exactly when you notified your professor.
